# eROSITA Globular Cluster Sources
Date: 2026-10-25

## eRASS1 Survey (Laktionov, Erlangen thesis)
- 39 globular clusters surveyed in eRASS1
- 113 X-ray sources detected total
- 16 brightest sources toward 9 clusters:
  - Liller 1
  - Terzan 2
  - NGC 1851
  - NGC 2808
  - Omega Centauri (NGC 5139)
  - NGC 6441
  - 47 Tuc (NGC 104)
  - M4 (NGC 6121)
  - NGC 4372
- 3 of 4 soft sources had membership confirmed via optical/IR color-magnitude diagrams

## 47 Tuc Dedicated Study (Saeedi et al. 2022)
- 5 eROSITA observations
- 888 point sources detected
- 14 classified as cluster members: 2 quiescent LMXBs, 4 cataclysmic variables
- No significant emission in 0.5-2 keV band

## Catalog Notes
- eRASS1 main catalog masked known globulars from galaxy-cluster search (clutter < 1 sq deg)
- No published eROSITA-blue straggler correlation yet

## Related Foundations
- Cheng et al.: Chandra survey of 69 clusters; weak-source emissivity scales with mass and encounter rate
- 2026 Nature study: 3000+ blue stragglers across 48 clusters; specific frequency anti-correlates with density and collision rate
