RULED-OUT REMNANT CANDIDATE:
NGC 6231 (open cluster): l=343.46, b=+1.186 — 0.6 kpc outside NGC 6397's 90% contour. Age 3-10 Myr consistent but position incompatible.

SEARCH CORRIDOR: galactic plane strip |b| < 5 deg, galactic longitude roughly 220-360 deg (the span covered by the six 90% contours). Best bets for young (<10 Myr) triggered star formation: NGC 6397 and NGC 3201 corridors — pre-main-sequence stars bright in IR (SPHEREx) and X-ray (eROSITA).

INCLINATION NOTES:
- High-inclination clusters (50-80 deg) cross the plane fast and at higher |z| velocity; their impact footprints are smaller on sky.
- Low-inclination M71 (15 deg) spends more time near the plane; larger footprint, more mixing with disk population.
- Retrograde NGC 3201's crossing geometry differs: relative velocity to disk gas is higher, shock compression stronger per Levy 2000.

CAVEATS: Contours dominated by Galactic potential model uncertainty (not just astrometry). Barred time-varying potentials (OrbIT 2025, arXiv:2512.06079) would shift and broaden these footprints further. No confirmed ISM or stellar remnant identified to date.
